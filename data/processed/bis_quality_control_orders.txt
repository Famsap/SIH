1. QUALITY CONTROL ORDERS (QCOS)
Statutory orders issued by Central Government Ministries under Section 16 of the BIS Act 2016 making BIS certification compulsory for notified products before manufacturing, selling, or importing into India. Non-compliance is punishable by imprisonment up to 2 years, heavy fines, and product seizure. Covers steel, cement, electrical appliances, toys, footwear, and chemicals.
