Blooms, Slabs Billets, Bars, Rings and are offered in various sizes as per national, international and Customers' specifications.
These high quality steels find applications in different sectors of industries like Automobile, Bearing, Engineering, Oil gas &
Mining, Railways etc. As India's leading alloy and special steel manufacturer, MSSSPL is the preferred supplier to many
multinational and domestic customers as well as the government for more than 50 years. Situated at Khopoli, Maharashtra
(India), and 90 km from Mumbai, adjacent to Mumbai - Pune National Highway. MSSSPL is spread over a plant area of 25
hectares. With ISO 9001: 2015, IATF 16949: 2016, ISO 45001, & ISO 50001 certifications, MSSSPL's vision is "To be the most
