Expression of Interest (EOI) for Setting up Assaying & Hallmarking Centre

under the Central Assistance Scheme

1. Bureau
of Indian Standards (BIS) invites applications from Central/State Government/
PSUs/Laboratories of repute in Public/Private sector/private entrepreneurs and other interested parties,
eliciting 'Expression of Interest' for 'Setting up of Gold Assaying and Hallmarking Centre' during
2021-22 and obtaining Central Assistance. The Assaying & Hallmarking (A&H) Centre shall be set up
in any District in India where no such A&H Centre exists.

2. Under the scheme entrepreneurs will be given one time financial assistance, the rates of which are given

below:

Terms and Conditions and who first obtains recognition by BIS will be eligible for the financial

All other centres even if they do not get the financial assistance, will be entitled to recognition by BIS as
Assaying & Hallmarking Centre, if found fulfilling the prescribed requirements.

4. A centre selected for central assistance, shall be required to operate as BIS Recognized Assaying and

Hallmarking Centre under BIS Hallmarking Scheme. For details of BIS Hallmarking Scheme please
visit our BIS Website: www.bis.gov.in .

5. The applicant centre or its promoters shall not have any linkage with Jewellery trade or manufacturing.

6. Application shall be made on the "Application Form for Expression of Interest for setting up Gold

Assaying/Hallmarking Centre under the Central Assistance Scheme" available on BIS website. The
applicable Terms and Conditions are also available on BIS website.
