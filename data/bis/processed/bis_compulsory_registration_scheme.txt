1. COMPULSORY REGISTRATION SCHEME (CRS)
Introduced under Scheme II of BIS Regulations for electronics, IT, and solar goods notified by MeitY and MNRE. Fast-track self-declaration of conformity based on laboratory test reports without requiring pre-license factory audit. Standard Registration Mark: 'Self Declaration - Conforming to IS ... R-XXXXXXXX'.
