NOTIFICATION

New Delhi, the 14th June , 2018

S.O 2421(E).-In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 14 of the Bureau of
Indian Standards Act, 2016 (11 of 2016), the Central Government, after consulting the Bureau of Indian
Standards, hereby notifies the following precious metal articles to be marked with Hallmark for the purposes
of the said sub-section, namely:-

1. Gold jewellery and gold artefacts
2. Silver jewellery and silver artefacts

[F.No. 6/1/2017-BIS]
