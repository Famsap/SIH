1. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQS) - BIS
A1: BIS is the National Standard Body of India under the BIS Act 2016 responsible for standards, testing, and product certification.
Q2: How do I verify an ISI mark on a product?
A2: Check the ISI monogram, the IS standard number on top, and the 7-8 digit CM/L license number at the bottom. Verify using the BIS Care App or Manakonline portal.
Q3: Is BIS certification mandatory?
A3: Mandatory for products covered under Government Quality Control Orders (QCOs) like steel, cement, electrical appliances, and infant nutrition.
Q4: What is HUID in Gold Hallmarking?
A4: Hallmark Unique Identification is a 6-digit laser-engraved alphanumeric code giving each gold jewellery item a traceable identity.
