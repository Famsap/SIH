1. OVERVIEW OF BIS PRODUCT CERTIFICATION SCHEME (ISI MARK)
The Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) operates a Product Certification Scheme under the BIS Act 2016 and Conformity Assessment Regulations 2018. The scheme grants a license to use the Standard Mark (ISI Mark) or a Certificate of Conformity to manufacturers whose products conform to applicable Indian Standards (IS).

2. SCHEME OBJECTIVES & CONFORMITY OPTIONS
- Scheme I (ISI Mark): Factory audit, testing of in-house manufacturing/QA facilities, and independent sample testing.
- Scheme II (Self-Declaration of Conformity): Compulsory Registration Scheme for electronics and IT goods.
- Scheme IV (Certificate of Conformity): Batch/lot inspection and test certificates.
- Foreign Manufacturers Certification Scheme (FMCS): For overseas production units exporting to India.

3. NORMAL APPLICATION PROCEDURE
1. Online Application: Submission of Form-V with technical documentation, factory layout, machinery, and test equipment list.
2. Preliminary Factory Inspection: Physical evaluation of manufacturing capability and quality control.
3. Sample Testing: Drawing of representative samples for testing in BIS-recognized labs.
4. Grant of License: Granted upon verification of test reports and factory compliance.
